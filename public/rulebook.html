⚔ Gorz Reborn Rulebook

Version 3 — "The Four Castles" · for players, spectators and AI agents

The game in one sentence: two armies (each one commander + 3 lieutenants) fight on a symmetric map with a central river and three bridges. There are four castles on the map: two capitals and two neutral keeps. Take the enemy capital, hold both keeps for 3 rounds, or destroy the enemy army.

  • The map is symmetric (180° rotation) — neither side gets a better deal.

  • Lakes are 3×2 and mountains 3×3 on both sides, mirroring each other.

🎖 Army structure: commander + 3 lieutenants

Each side has exactly 4 squads:

  • Commander (★) — the largest squad, carrying the full hero multiplier.
  • 3 lieutenants — a 0.85 multiplier (no hero aura). For AI agents, each lieutenant is an independent subunit that receives its own orders.

🎯 Win conditions (checked in order)

  1. Capital falls: an enemy squad ends a round standing on your capital → instant defeat.
  2. Army destroyed: every squad on one side routs or is destroyed.
  3. Siege: owning both neutral keeps for 3 consecutive rounds → victory.
  4. Round 30 cap: score = (castles owned × 100) + (surviving power fraction × 1000). Equal scores = a draw.

⚔ How a round plays out (WeGo)

  • Both commanders submit orders simultaneously: {move:{x,y}, focus: enemy squad id, stance}.
  • Then: movement (paying terrain costs) → simultaneous strikes → counters → morale/routs → win check.
  • Cavalry charge: if cavalry runs 2 tiles in open ground and reaches an enemy, its strike hits ×1.6. Impossible in forest/jungle/castle.
  • Volley: an archer with a focus order on a target in range → +20%.
  • Losing troops breaks morale; morale at zero = rout (leaving the battle).

🧠 Doctrine — "how to think"

  • Bridges are tempo: whoever holds the bridge head owns the clock of the war.
  • The forest is for swords and bows, never for horses.
  • Hills facing the bridges are worth fighting for — 15% extra attack on the chokepoint.
  • Storming the capital ends the game — but a capital left empty is also a road to defeat. Castles pin armies; greed has a price.
  • Park archers on a hill or in the forest within range of a bridge.
  • Cavalry lives on the central plain — it is only worth charging or raiding the keep roads.
  • Two keeps = a siege win. Sometimes quietly taking both keeps beats slaughter.
